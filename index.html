PROJECT FAIL MARY

Fix the ship before it blows. It will take a Hail Mary.
Aim: mouse, keys or stick. Hold the grab key or left click to grab a wall, release to push off toward your aim, hold longer for a harder push. SHIFT or right click: thrusters. Hold the repair key near a broken panel. Press Enter in a lobby or game to chat. Change any key with the cog at the top.
Gamepad in game: stick aim, A grab, RT thrust, Y repair, X drop, LT share fuel. Select or View opens chat. Start opens the emote wheel, then aim with the stick or D-pad and press A. In menus: D-pad or stick to move, A select, B back, Start confirm.
